Description field

Having an identity crisis? Voicebound has you covered.

Your Troll has always felt like a Dwarf on the inside. Your Undead misses the big, booming laugh of a Tauren. Your Gnome would like to sound seven feet tall, just once.

Voicebound lets your character speak with any other race's voice. No race change, no barber, no paid service, no therapy needed.

What gets the makeover

  • Every "I can't do that" moment. "Not enough energy!", "Not enough mana", "Out of range", "That ability isn't ready yet", "I have no target", "My inventory is full"... about 49 lines in all.
  • Your emotes. /laugh, /cheer, /cry, /sigh, /joke, /thank, /bye, /no and /nod, plus the battle calls /attacktarget, /incoming, /flee and /healme.

Find your true voice in 30 seconds

  1. Click the Voicebound button on your minimap (or type /rvs).
  2. Pick a race and gender.
  3. Tick Enable Voice Override. That's it.

Not sure yet? Hit Test Voice to audition every line before you commit. Changed your mind? Right-click the minimap button to switch back instantly.

Voices: Human, Orc, Dwarf, Night Elf, Undead, Tauren, Gnome and Troll, male or female.

The fine print

  • The real voices. Original Blizzard recordings only. No AI voices, nothing fake.
  • Your secret is safe. Only you hear the new voice. Everyone else still hears your character's normal one.
  • No commitment. It lasts for your play session. Log out and you're yourself again.
  • Nothing to install. No game files changed, no extra programs.
  • Combat grunts and death cries stay the same. The game gives addons no way to change those.

Commands

  • /rvs or /voicebound: open the window
  • /rvs on, /rvs off: voice override on or off
  • /rvs race dwarf, /rvs gender female: choose a voice
  • /rvs reset: back to your own voice
  • /rvs minimap: hide or show the minimap button
